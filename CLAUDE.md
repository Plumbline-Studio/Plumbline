# Plumbline

## Plumbline Brand Architecture (canonical)

**Plumbline Studio** is the parent company and the "build it true" philosophy, and the
services arm for established businesses. Its products are separate doors under it:
- **Toolwright** — startups & individuals; the entry point; seminars on building with AI (toolwright.dev)
- **Old World Trade** — artisans
- **Breakpoint** — churches
- *(TBD)* — healthcare / chronic-illness

There is NO "Tool Maker" master brand above Plumbline — Plumbline is the top.
Philosophy through-line: build true, well-made tools that should exist, for people
already worthy of their path; meet people where they are. Grow one product at a time
(Pieter Levels model): one live and earning before the next.
**This repo's place:** Plumbline Studio — the parent company brand and site.

## Owner-operator growth thesis (2026-10-28)

**Canonical reference:** [`docs/strategy/2026-10-28-owner-capacity-thesis.md`](docs/strategy/2026-10-28-owner-capacity-thesis.md).

Plumbline Studio builds systems so owner-operators can decentralize themselves from daily operations and businesses can grow beyond the owner's personal capacity. **Growth is the opportunity; automation is a tool; organizational capability is the outcome; freedom is a benefit.** The goal is to spread knowledge, routine decisions and execution while retaining owner visibility and appropriate control. Do not equate this with replacing owners, mandatory AI, or unlimited business growth. This is an internal strategic lens, **not authorization** to change the current bounded-offer storefront, pricing, scope or client promises without Kyle's approval.
