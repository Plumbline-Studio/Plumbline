How it works

You are my client. Your client stays yours. You contract with them and set your own price.

01Bring one build

One bounded problem for one of your clients. Name the client before we talk, and they are registered to you.

02Scope in two days

A 30-minute scoping call (your client joins only if you want). Within 2 business days: a one-page scope with the outcome, the acceptance test, the trade price, the start week and what is not included.

03I build, you're informed

You sign the scope and pay. I build. You get a written status note every week.

04Handoff you can present

Plain-language docs and a short walkthrough you can present as your own.

Money

Fixed price per build, quoted on the scope.

Trade rate · 15% below published price · billed in advance

  • Trade rate. 15% below Plumbline's published price for the same work (published range $2,500–$5,000 for a 2–4 week build). Larger work is split into more than one fixed-price build.
  • You set your client's price. I never see, set or limit it.
  • Billed in advance. Always. The full build price is paid before work starts. No net terms.
  • Changes go in writing: I quote time and price, you approve in writing and pay, then work starts. The price never drifts silently.
  • Outside costs (hosting, paid APIs, seats) are named in the scope and paid by the end client directly or passed through at cost.
  • No referral fees and no revenue share, in either direction.

Your client stays yours

No poaching

I will not sell to, or take work directly from, your client for 12 months after the last build for them, unless you agree in writing. If your client contacts me directly, I tell you the same day and send them back to you.

Your brand or mine

Your choice on each build: white-label (you present it as your work; I'm not named to the client) or co-branded ("Built by Plumbline"). I don't name you, your client or the work in my marketing without your written OK.

You own the build

Once paid, the deliverables for that build are yours (or your client's, as you direct). I keep my pre-existing tools and methods and license them to you inside the build, perpetually and royalty-free.

Confidential

Your client's identity, your methods and pricing, and the arrangement itself stay confidential. A mutual NDA before the first scoping call when you or your client need one.

Support

Reply within one business day. 30 days of fixes after handoff for anything that fails the agreed acceptance test. Ongoing support is quoted separately, billed in advance, and yours to resell.

Plain limits

Liability is capped at the fees paid for that build. Either side can end the partnership with 30 days' written notice; paid work is finished and handed off.

Pilot first, terms after

We start with one client and one small, defined build, paid in advance at trade rate. If it works, we sign a short build-partner agreement with these terms.

I'm one builder. Builds are scheduled in the order payments arrive, the scope states the start week, and I'll tell you up front when the queue is full.
