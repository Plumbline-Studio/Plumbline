The steps

Four steps, the same every time

1. Bring one stuck thing

Bring your Orchestrator snapshot, or just tell me. If you work for a client, tell me who. From then on, they are yours, and I will not go around you.

2. We scope it

We talk for 30 minutes. Your Visionary or your client joins only if you want. Then you get one page: what I build, what "done" means, the price and the start week.

3. You pay, I build

You sign the page and pay. Then I build and test. You get a short note in writing every week.

4. You hand it over

You get the tool, plain notes and a short walkthrough. If you work for a client, you can show it as your own.

Whose name is on it

Your name, both names, or I work beside you

This part is for fractional Integrators, consultants and firms who build for their own clients.

Your brand

You show it as your work. Your client never hears my name.

Both names

It says “Built by Plumbline” next to your name.

Beside you

I meet your client as part of your team. This is billed by the hour. The client is still yours.

You pick for each job. I never name you, your client or the work in my ads unless you say yes in writing.

Money

A price on every page, paid first

  • Each job has its own price. It is on the one-page scope, before you say yes.
  • You pay before work starts. No bills later. No surprise totals.
  • Working beside you is billed by the hour. You get an estimate of the hours first.
  • You set your client's price. I never see it or set it.
  • Changes go in writing first. I give the new time and price. You say yes and pay. Then work starts.
  • Outside costs are named up front. Things like hosting are paid by your client, or passed on at cost.
  • No referral fees and no revenue share. Not in either direction.
  • Need help after a build? Ask on the call.

The relationship

Your client stays yours

Implementers keep coaching

If an EOS Implementer sent you, they stay your coach. I build. I don't teach EOS or coach your team.

I won't go around you

For 12 months after the last job, I won't sell to your client or take work from them, unless you say yes in writing. If they call me, I tell you that day and send them back to you.

You own the build

Once you pay, what I built for that job is yours, or your client's if you say so. I keep my own tools and let you use them inside the build, free, for good.

It stays private

Your client's name, your methods, your prices and our deal stay private. We can sign an NDA before the first call.

Help when you need it

I reply within one business day. For 30 days after handover, I fix anything that fails the test we agreed on.

Plain limits

The most I owe on a job is what you paid for it. Either of us can stop with 30 days' notice in writing. Paid work still gets finished.
