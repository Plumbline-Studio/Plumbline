What this is

The other case studies on this site are work for clients. This one is the system underneath them: how I decide what to build, how "done" is agreed and checked, and the tools and AI workers that keep a one-person studio honest and on track.

It is a work in progress, and it shows its own records as they are. The map below scores every part twice: how much is built, and how many of its written conditions of "done" are met. Where those two disagree, the records are behind the work, and that is shown too.

The four parts in this series

  • The method: one template every project starts from, with a yes-or-no check before building, a written definition of done, and one automated gate.
  • The Factory: one container for the whole studio, five of nine phases built.
  • Plumb-bob: a checker that asks "does it work, does it match, should it exist?", released and still being made reliable.
  • The AI staff: workers with written contracts, and a memory that carries each session into the next.

How the parts connect

The Factory sits in the middle. Most other parts either move into it or depend on it: the AI staff move in at its Phase 6, the knowledge index and brand kit at Phase 5, client work at Phase 7. The studio's database is being adopted by the Factory and retired as a separate service at Phase 8. Plumb-bob's results already feed the Factory's QA runs. The method is the same everywhere: the template's gate, playscripts with an integrity veto, blueprints, and the rule that nothing is sent in my name.
