09 / Questions

What Integrators ask first.

Do you teach EOS or coach the team?

No. That is your EOS Implementer's work. I build, and I give plain business advice when it helps the build.

Who keeps the relationship?

Your Implementer keeps the coaching. You work with me on the build, and I keep them posted if you want me to.

Will you change our EOS tools?

No. I feed the tools you already use, so the numbers and steps show up there.

What does the Orchestrator cost?

It is free. Say what is stuck, and you get a map and up to three next moves. If that gets you there, you are done.

What does a build cost?

It depends on the build. After a 30 minute call, you get one page with the plan and the price. You pay before I start.
