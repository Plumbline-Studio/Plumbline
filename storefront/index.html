One small change.
A better way to run.

Customer order confirmation

A due date that waits until the order is ready.

Before

The tracker set every due date the day the deposit cleared, before anyone knew what was ordered or where it shipped. Customers confirmed their details on a PDF.

After

Customers confirm their order in a private portal, and the lead time starts the day they do. Until then the date reads "Set on confirmation", for them and for the shop.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need to know what to build?

No. Bring the work that eats your week. Finding the right fix is what the call and the diagnostic are for.

Do I have to replace my spreadsheet?

Usually not. Most builds start from the sheet or system you already run and make it do more.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the build. The schedule is agreed with the price before work starts, with your review time and any outside dependencies counted in.

Who owns it afterwards?

You do, once it's paid for, and it can run in accounts you own. Running costs are named in the scope.
