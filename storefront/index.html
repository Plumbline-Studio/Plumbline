A real build

A due date that waits for the customer

A commercial fabricator. Client anonymized until they sign off.

Before

Every job got a due date the day the deposit cleared, before anyone knew what was ordered or where it shipped. The shop planned against dates that were going to slip, and customers confirmed their details on a PDF.

After

Each customer gets a private link to their own order and confirms the details there. The lead time starts the day they confirm; until then the date reads "Set on confirmation", for the customer and the shop alike.

39 of 39 open jobs kept their due date to the day when the change went live on the shop's tracker.
