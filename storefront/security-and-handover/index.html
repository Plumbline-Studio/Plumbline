Security and handover

What's agreed before
anything is built.

Every build's scope says, in writing, where it runs, what data it touches, who can get in, how AI is used, and what you get at the end. This page lists those items, and the rules that hold on every project. If you're an IT provider vetting me for a client, start here.

Agreed per project

Written into every scope

Hosting

Where it runs, whose account it's in, and what that costs each month. Where possible it runs in accounts you or your client own.

Data

What information the tool stores or passes through, where it lives, and how long it's kept. Sensitive data is named, not assumed.

Access

Who can sign in to what, at which level, and how my own access is handled during the build and after handover.

AI use

Which AI services, if any, may touch your data, and for what. If the answer is none, the scope says none.

Your standards

If your team or your client has rules for passwords, backups, logging or vendors, they go into the scope and the "done" list.

Handover

What you receive at the end: the running tool, the accounts, plain-language notes and a walkthrough.

On every project

Rules that don't change

No secrets in code

Passwords and API keys live in the hosting provider's settings, never in the code or its history.

Access checked before launch

Who can see what is set up per person and checked before the tool goes live.

A person approves every change

AI drafts and checks code; every change passes automated checks and a person's review before it's merged.

Nothing sent in your name

No email, post or payment goes to your customers or staff because an AI decided to. Sending is a person's decision.

Live data touched last

Changes to live systems are planned step by step, with a backup first. See a real switch-on plan →

What I don't have

I'm one builder, not a security firm. I don't hold a SOC 2 report or other certification. If your client requires one, or has a security questionnaire, say so on the first call. I'll answer it honestly, or tell you this isn't the right fit.
