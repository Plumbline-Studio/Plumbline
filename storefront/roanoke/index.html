Questions

Asked by local owners

Will you come to my business?
Yes. I'm based in Roanoke and meet clients in person across the Roanoke Valley, Lynchburg and the New River Valley. The first call is by phone or video; after that we can sit down at your shop or office.
Do I have to be in the Roanoke area?
No. I build for businesses anywhere in the United States, remotely. Being local just means we can also meet in person.
Do I have to replace the tools I already use?
Usually not. Most builds work inside what you already have. If something has to change, the scope says so before you pay for the build.
What does it cost?
A diagnostic is $500 to $2,000 and is credited toward the build. A build is $2,500 to $15,000, agreed before work starts. A care plan afterwards is optional, $300 to $2,000 a month.
Who owns it when you're done?
You do. The tool, the accounts it runs in and the notes on how it works are handed over, and the scope says so in writing. What's agreed in writing →
