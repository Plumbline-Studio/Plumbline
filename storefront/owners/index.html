Questions

Before you book

Do I need to know what to build?

No. Bring the work that eats your week. The call and the first step are for finding the right fix.

Do I have to give up my spreadsheet?

Most of the time, no. Most builds start from the sheet or system you already use, and make it do more.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the build. We agree on the dates with the price, before I start.

Who owns it when you're done?

You do, once it is paid for. It can run in accounts you own. Running costs are named up front.
