On every job

Rules that don't change

No secrets in code

Passwords and keys live in the host's settings. They are never in the code or its history.

Access checked before launch

Who can see what is set up for each person, and checked before the tool goes live.

A person says yes to every change

AI drafts and checks code. Every change passes automatic checks and a person's review before it goes in.

Nothing sent in your name

No email, post or payment goes to your customers or staff because an AI decided to. A person decides.
